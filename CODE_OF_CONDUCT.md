# Code of conduct

OptiTray follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, whether in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews, should be able to
do so without harassment, whatever their background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@pigeonboy-cmyk](https://github.com/pigeonboy-cmyk), for example by
asking in a new issue for a private way to get in touch (without giving details there). You can also use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may
warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. The response depends on the severity
and on whether the behaviour continues after a warning.
